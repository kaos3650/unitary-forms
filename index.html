Unitary modular forms for U(2) — pieces matched to LMFDB newforms

Fields done so far: all with hK ≤ 3, and hK = 4 up to dK = −312 (plus −323, I2, weight [0,0]) (computations skipped by the time limits are not listed). Lattices I2 and diag(1,2), weights [0,0], [2,0], [3,1]; classical weight k = a − b + 2. Each Hecke-irreducible piece is matched with PARI against every newform of weight k and level dividing 4|dK|, at all good primes 3 ≤ p < 30: inert λ = ap2/pk−2 − (p+1); split (weight 0) λ2n = ap2n, n = exponent of Cl(K); split (higher weight, 1-dim pieces) N(λ) = ap2/pk−2. These rules do not see twists by genus characters, so twist-equivalent forms are listed together, smallest level first. Labels follow LMFDB ordering (orbits sorted by trace form). Bianchi links open the LMFDB list of Bianchi forms over K (base change of the weight-2 form is expected there).
